# Code of Conduct

WDMWD is an open-source project. Contributors are expected to help maintain a respectful, constructive, and welcoming environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique code, ideas, and decisions without attacking people.
- Explain disagreement clearly and stay focused on the technical issue.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or intimidation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults targeting personal characteristics, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Project moderation

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when behaviour violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an existing private contact channel. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
